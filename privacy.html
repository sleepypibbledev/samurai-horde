SAMURAI HORDE

Privacy Policy

Effective 25 November 2026 · Version 1.0

Short version: Samurai Horde keeps your progress on your device. There are no accounts, no ads and no analytics in the current version, and we do not collect, share or sell personal data.

1. Who we are

Samurai Horde (“the Game”) is published by Sleepy Pibble. Contact: SleepyPibbleDev@gmail.com.

2. Data stored on your device

Your level, coins, crystals, upgrades, heroes, skins, quests, settings and a randomly generated player name are saved in the app storage on your device. This data is not sent to us or to anyone else. Clearing the app data or uninstalling the Game deletes it.

3. What we do not do

  • No accounts, sign-in or social features.
  • No advertising SDK and no analytics or crash-reporting SDK in the current version.
  • No collection, sharing or sale of personal data.
  • The weekly tournament uses computer-controlled rivals; nothing about you is sent online.

4. Purchases

In the current test version all offers are free and no payments are processed. If real in-app purchases are added later, they will be processed by Google Play, we will only receive confirmation that a purchase was completed, and this policy will be updated before that version is released.

5. Advertising

The current version shows no real advertisements. If an advertising network is added later, this policy will be updated before that version is released and will describe the data that network processes.

6. Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone.

7. Retention and deletion

Local data stays on your device until you reset or uninstall the Game. We hold no server-side user data, so there is nothing for us to delete; you can still write to us with any question.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
